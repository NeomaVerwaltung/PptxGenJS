# Draft: Contributor Rights Agreement under German Law

> **DRAFT — FOR LEGAL REVIEW ONLY.** This document is not in effect, does not
> amend the [MIT License](./LICENSE), and does not bind NEOMA GmbH or any
> contributor. Each contributor must sign an approved, completed agreement
> before it can apply to a contribution.

## 1. Parties and Contribution

This agreement is between NEOMA GmbH ("NEOMA") and the individual or entity
identified as the contributor ("Contributor"). It applies only to the specific
contribution identified in the completed agreement, including its pull request
or commit reference (the "Contribution").

## 2. Grant of Rights

To the extent that the Contributor owns or controls rights in the Contribution,
the Contributor grants NEOMA an exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, transferable,
sublicensable right to use the Contribution in every **currently known** form
of use. This includes the right to reproduce, modify, translate, combine,
publish, distribute, make available to the public, license, sub-license,
commercialise, and enforce rights in the Contribution.

The Contributor consents, to the extent permitted by law, to NEOMA modifying,
combining, publishing, and distributing the Contribution with or without
attribution.

## 3. Contributor Assurances

The Contributor confirms that the Contribution is original to the Contributor,
or that the Contributor has obtained all rights and permissions needed to make
this grant. The Contributor will identify in writing any third-party material,
including its licence terms, contained in the Contribution.

## 4. Legal Limits

Under German law, copyright itself remains with the author. This agreement is
therefore a grant of exclusive rights of use, not a transfer of authorship.
Rights in future or currently unknown forms of use are not granted by this
draft and require a separate agreement in the form required by law.

Nothing in this agreement limits non-waivable statutory rights or mandatory
remuneration claims.

## 5. Approval and Execution

This draft becomes effective only after it has been completed for the specific
Contribution and signed by both parties in a form approved by NEOMA's legal
department. A pull request, commit, issue comment, or other repository action
does not by itself accept this agreement.
